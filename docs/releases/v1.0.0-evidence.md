## Known limitations

- Production history remains short; reliability objectives that need longer history remain `INSUFFICIENT_HISTORY`.
- The deployment uses Vercel Hobby and Neon Free; resource limits and policy changes remain external constraints.
- AI providers are optional and quota-bound. Groq has one bounded persisted production smoke; Gemini is not live-verified.
- The local benchmark is a Windows/PostgreSQL fixture baseline, not a cross-hardware performance claim.
- The product is batch-oriented and intentionally does not include streaming, authentication, notifications, autonomous remediation, or additional data sources.
